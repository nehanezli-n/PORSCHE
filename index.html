    WEISSACH DEVELOPMENT CENTRE

    Porsche Engineering Architecture

    1. Central Single-Radiator Concept

    Directly adopted from the 911 RSR and 963 Le Mans race cars. Instead of three radiators, a large angled center radiator in the nose frees up side space for active aerodynamic elements.

    2. Drag Reduction System (DRS)

    For the first time on a road-legal Porsche, DRS flattens the wings within milliseconds at the touch of a button on the steering wheel, reducing drag for higher straight-line velocities.

    3. Double-Wishbone Front Axle with Teardrop Profiles

    Because the wheel arches are subjected to powerful airflows, the double-wishbone suspension links are shaped as aerodynamic teardrop profiles, generating up to 40 kg of front downforce at top speed.
